# PromoVideoHub — Quick Start Guide
### AI Video Generator — User Manual

---

## 🔐 Step 1: Log In

1. Open the app in your browser: **https://promovideohub.com**
2. You'll see a login screen that says **"Video Engine Access"**
3. Enter the access key: `cshvideo2026`
4. Click **"Unlock Engine"**

---

## 🎬 Step 2: Create a Video

Once logged in, you'll see the **Dashboard** with a form on the left.

### Option A: Let AI Do Everything (Recommended)
1. Click the **✨ Auto Fill** button (top-right of the form)
2. Wait 2-3 seconds — AI will fill in everything for you
3. Review the generated idea and tweak if needed
4. Click **▶ Generate Video**

### Option B: Fill It In Yourself
Fill in these fields:

| Field | What to enter | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Video Title** | A short catchy title (max 8 words) | `The Future of AI in 2026` |
| **Topic / Prompt** | Describe what the video should cover (2-3 sentences) | `Explore how AI is reshaping industries from healthcare to finance. Focus on the most surprising developments and what experts predict for the next decade.` |
| **Duration** | How long the video should be | `60 Seconds` is a good start |
| **Visual Style** | The look and feel of the video | See style options below |
| **Background Music** | Pick a music track (or leave default) | Click to preview tracks |

Then click **▶ Generate Video**.

---

## 🎨 Visual Style Options

| Style | Best For |
|---|---|
| **Documentary** | Informational, news-style content |
| **Cyberpunk** | Tech, futuristic, sci-fi topics |
| **Minimalist** | Clean, professional presentations |
| **Cinematic 8k** | High-impact, movie-like content |
| **Animated / Cartoon** | Fun, explainer-style videos |
| **Nature Documentary** | Wildlife, environment, outdoors |
| **Tech Review** | Product reviews, gadgets, software |
| **Horror / Dark** | Mystery, thriller, spooky topics |

---

## ⏳ Step 3: Wait for Rendering

After clicking Generate:
- You'll see a **blue "Queued"** badge appear at the top
- It changes to **yellow "Rendering..."** when processing starts
- Then **green "Completed"** when done
- Typical render time: **2-5 minutes** depending on duration

---

## 📥 Step 4: Watch & Download

Once completed, your video appears in the **"Generated Videos"** section below the form.

- **Hover** over a video thumbnail to preview it
- Click the **👁 Eye icon** to watch in fullscreen
- Click the **⬇ Download icon** to save the MP4 file
- Click the **🗑 Trash icon** to delete it

---

## 🎵 Music Preview

When selecting background music:
- Click the **▶ Play** button next to each track to preview
- Tracks are automatically filtered by your selected style
- The selected track will have a **purple highlight**

---

## ℹ️ Dashboard Info

On the right side you'll see:
- **System Overview** — Total jobs, completed, in queue, failed
- **Engine Status** — Shows if all AI services are online (green = good)
- **Open Full Video Library** — Browse all generated videos in one page

---

## 💡 Tips

- **Start with 60 seconds** — It's the sweet spot for quality and speed
- **Use Auto Fill first** — Then customize the idea to your liking
- **Be specific in the topic** — More detail = better AI-generated content
- **Try different styles** — Each style gives a completely different look
- **The engine must show "Online"** — If it says "Offline", the server needs to be started
